* Calicut: 69 phone numbers you added were lost

Between 2 and 6 October you added 69 missing phone numbers, but the day's list was refreshed from fets.live again and again. Each refresh copied the original blank numbers back over yours, along with exam parts and corrected names. Those numbers cannot be recovered. There is no need to refresh a day's list more than once: no new exams are added on the same day. From the next report onwards, the numbers you add will be kept and counted.
